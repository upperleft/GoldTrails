# Your Camp: Connections visibility

At the user's explicit request, Your Camp does not mention Connections for a registered free member. The shortcut, sidebar navigation entry, and support-panel mention appear only with an effective subscriber entitlement. Missing entitlement and expired membership (raw subscriber label but effective free level) fail closed.

The account response remains private/no-store and uses the existing server-resolved membership. This change does not alter the destination's existing access enforcement or public creator resources. It is scoped to Your Camp; the broader navigation review remains outstanding.
