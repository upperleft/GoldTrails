# River clothing article and product research

Prepared November 1, 2026. Develop a practical clothing guide alongside the Gold Trails launch work, then expand the catalog using verified manufacturer information and clearly attributed creator experience.

## Article brief

Working title: **What to wear for a day prospecting beside a river**.

Help a newcomer understand the clothing choices before buying equipment. Explain the difference between stockingfoot waders requiring separate footwear, bootfoot waders, hip waders and ordinary water footwear. Cover fit, layers and socks, gloves for different tasks, rain and sun clothing, care between trips and a short packing checklist. Include options for different body sizes and women's fits.

Link relevant existing products and the future first outing guide. Keep shopping guidance distinct from advice about whether a river is safe to enter. Review [National Park Service river safety](https://www.nps.gov/articles/river-and-stream-safety.htm), [US Fish and Wildlife Service river crossing guidance](https://www.fws.gov/story/how-cross-river), manufacturer instructions and location-specific rules before drafting safety or biosecurity claims.

Proposed outline:

1. Plan the day and decide what conditions the clothing must suit.
2. Understand wader styles and the separate role of boots.
3. Check fit, layers and socks together.
4. Match gloves and outer clothing to the actual task and weather.
5. Pack a change of clothing and review care instructions.
6. Compare the sourced catalog examples and note what remains unverified.
