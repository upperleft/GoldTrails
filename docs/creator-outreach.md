## Preparation and automation

1. Check the creator's identity, profile URL and suitable creator-owned contact source. Record the source URL and date; do not infer an address from a name or use order-only contacts as general outreach routes.
2. Confirm the sending identity and reply inbox. The Hostinger receiving mailbox and Resend's authenticated outgoing sender are separate. Test delivery and replies using the intended sender before the first creator invitation.
3. Prepare one reviewed message per creator, with a personal line and correct profile. Generate account invitations only after the acceptance flow passes its tests.
4. Track draft, sent, replied, accepted, declined, opted out and delivery failure as separate states. Provider acceptance of an email does not prove inbox delivery or invitation acceptance.
5. For later automation, add a private queue with a unique creator/recipient/campaign key, delivery identifiers and retry handling. Recheck sent state before retrying so a timeout does not produce duplicate invitations. Keep raw invitation tokens and URLs out of logs and public files.
6. Stop pending follow-ups when someone replies, declines or opts out, and suppress bounced recipients. Choose a follow-up cadence before enabling automatic follow-ups.

Existing research snapshots remain evidence, not a send queue. The [creator invitation workspace](creator-invitations.md) generates personal links and copyable messages.
