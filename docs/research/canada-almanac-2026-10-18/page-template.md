# Reusable Canadian guide template

Research schema, not a published website page. Use the site’s established page layout during a later import; no redesign is implied.

# {Province or territory}: gold prospecting research

{Two short sentences describing the documented setting without promising finds or entry.}

## Before you go

{Current equipment pathway, tenure check, surface/Indigenous permissions, protected-land restrictions and relevant water approvals. Cite each condition; show actual date checked. Avoid an unqualified “panning is legal.”}

## Gold geology

{Types and documented districts. Separate bedrock ore from placer gold. A local grain-size observation stays local; leave a province-wide typical-size field absent if unsupported.}

## Mining history and discoveries

{Cited events with original contributor credits. Preserve disputed dates as research notes rather than flatten conflicting accounts.}

## Production in context

{Only statistics with unit, period, definition, geography and qualifier. Label annual/preliminary/mine/camp totals visibly. If no meaningful statistic is verified, omit the public statistic block.}

## Seasons and conditions

{Local climate, thaw, flow and travel evidence. State whether advice is an interpretation. Keep regulated operating dates separate from practical visitor seasons. No unsupported universal best-month window.}

## Rocks and minerals

{Deposit-specific examples, their limits and any contamination concern. Indicators are clues, not proof of gold.}

## Maps and research

{Official surveys, map scales, databases and downloadable reports. Distinguish interactive tools from documents and legacy datasets from current tenure.}

## Creators, clubs and further learning

{Reuse canonical existing records and public profile links by IDs. Never duplicate a creator or imply a video establishes permission. Include only independently verified video claims/timestamps. Public profiles remain free.}

## Sources and dates

{Fact-linked descriptive URLs, author, publication/update date when known, actual source check date. No inferred upload dates.}
